Dream Destinations

Travel planning website

A website that covers everything about a place you want to visit, built around one idea: get the traveller ready before they land.

HTML/CSSPHPMySQLApache

Most travel sites are built to sell you a package. This one is built to prepare you. It walks you from continent, to country, to the detail you actually need on the ground: what the food is like, which places are worth the trip, how to move around, and where to stay.

How you move through it

The part that actually mattered

The feedback form is the piece I care about most. Every submission is stored in a database behind an Apache server, which means feedback becomes a dataset rather than a pile of emails, and you can look at it to work out what travellers keep asking for.
