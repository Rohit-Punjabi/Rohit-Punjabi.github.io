Inventory Analytics & Demand Forecasting

Wholesale clothing retailer · shipped as a desktop app
PythonPandasstatsmodels PlotlyStreamlitExcel

Business question

What is actually selling, what is dead stock, and how much of each product should the store order in the coming months?

Data

About 50 monthly Excel workbooks spanning 4+ years: inconsistent column names, quantities stored as text, and a product naming change that split sales histories in two.

Approach

Built a Pandas pipeline that unifies every workbook into one tidy table with automated data health checks, matches renamed products by style code, selects the best forecast model per product, and validates accuracy by backtesting on held out months. Forecasts feed safety stock, reorder points, and a monthly order plan.

Key insight

The data health checks caught quantities saved as text like "55 PCS", which had silently hidden about 38% of all sales and distorted which products looked like best sellers.
